# Publication Readiness Reference

Use this reference for article/manuscript readiness audits across scholarly project repos.

## Evidence Sources

- Manuscript frontmatter and body.
- Cover letter or submission letter.
- Readiness checklist/report.
- Project hub files such as `ARTICLES`, GTD, roadmap, or publication plan.
- `.ai_state.md` recent completed work and open gates.
- Repo rules such as `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, or validation docs.

## Audit Checklist

- Readiness score and status wording agree across trackers.
- Title, article ID, target journal, and submission stage are consistent.
- Author name/byline form matches journal requirements.
- ORCID and email are present where required and match the canonical source.
- Open gates are explicit, countable, and not silently closed.
- Bibliography, forbidden strings, and repo-specific validation rules pass.
- Generated artifacts are refreshed or listed as follow-up.

## Verdicts

- `ready`: all required evidence is consistent and validation passes.
- `not ready`: known gates remain and are documented.
- `inconsistent`: trackers disagree; list exact conflicts.
- `blocked`: needs author/editor/archive/external decision.

## Handoff Shape

Report article ID, target journal, files checked, verdict, validation command/result, closed gates, remaining gates, and exact next action.

## Example Tasks

- Audit whether A21 is still 5/5 after roadmap edits.
- Verify ORCID/email/byline consistency before journal submission.
- Decide whether a manuscript can move from draft to submission-staged.
